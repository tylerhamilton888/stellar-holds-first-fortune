# First-hour presentation — 0.122.0

The opening begins with the parents’ purchased holding. Ivar introduces the same Rise near arrival; the player can use the modest cottage, read the parent letter and choose a personal ambition before mining. The brief scene is saved/skippable, and existing fortunes retain their property and progress. See [implementation and migration](HOME169.md).

The first stone load remains a paid practical introduction, with its original once-only reward. It no longer gates ownership or civic work. The journal and HUD follow the selected home, mining, forge or trade lead. Two civic projects or trade seals earn household recognition; adding light to the starter cottage can satisfy the later improved-home milestone without rebuilding.

Lodge conversations retain the lower-screen portrait and scrolling choices. Ivar recognizes paid work even when the property tour has not been taken. The parent letter remains available in the journal. Accepted rescue progress takes precedence over the opening, and later campaign acts remain outside the default early view.

Forge guidance distinguishes inspection, gathering or buying the remaining coal, saved partial delivery and returning for the first firing. Personal coal processing is optional. Default work lessons only take over the HUD when the player selects work guidance; other ambitions retain campaign guidance. Existing owners validate costs, local transactions and once-only rewards.
