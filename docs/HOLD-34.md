# Keldrath district navigation

Three walkable top-down floor plans connect Crownward, Hearthward and Forgeward. WASD moves freely within the paved galleries and central courts. Swept movement and building collision prevent crossing walls. E enters nearby doors, uses stairs, or opens lift destination selection. G toggles an illustrated cutaway overview; clicking a destination marks it without teleporting the player.

Doors resolve to existing local interiors. Map entry records the floor position; leaving restores that doorway. The concourse remains a playable side-view street. King's Gate connects regional travel. Indoor maps are survey-only. Audience, training and first-expedition quest gates remain enforced.

The version-2 hold_travel save records floor coordinates and the nearest stable node. Invalid floor positions fall back to a valid landing. Older graph saves resolve to their landing. Each tier currently shares a handcrafted circulation pattern with different destinations and landmarks; Keldrath is not procedurally generated. Background rooms are scenery. Character art uses existing side-facing walk cycles, including while moving north/south.

Future holds can supply different floor geometry and stable destination IDs. Retain generation versions and local changes in each saved hold. Local rooms, hold travel, regional travel and future stellar navigation should remain distinct scales sharing destination and return-location conventions.
