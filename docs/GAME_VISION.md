# Game vision
The Stellar Holds is an original Dwarven science-fantasy guided sandbox. Begin as a young prospector in Keldrath, earn a claim, build an outpost and crew, and work toward a first ship. The intended full-game progression extends through a clan, company, corporation, nation and galactic empire. These later scales are a vision, not delivered gameplay.

Hands-on mining must remain enjoyable after automation becomes powerful: read geology, follow seams, uncover caves, make satisfying physical cuts, and build useful routes. Crafting, exploration and hired industry coexist. Common materials retain practical and economic uses at every scale.

The eventual galaxy contains procedural worlds and civilizations, trading, bargaining, markets, diplomacy and acquisition. Dynasties and descendants grow around an enduring, eventually immortal Greatbeard. The immortality mechanism and inheritance rules remain to be designed. Worldforging, engineering whole artificial worlds, is uniquely Dwarven; ordinary stations and habitats need not be.

Visual identity: charming original 2D/cartoon and pixel-art-inspired characters; Celtic/Norse ornament; grounded monumental holds; gradual industrial, scientific and rune-tech progression. Portraits and cinematic paintings complement properly proportioned world sprites. Tools must visibly connect to hands and hit with their working ends. Original visual/audio assets and preserved provenance are required; references are inspiration, not borrowed game content.
