# 0.112.0 — Freight through the holding

In **O → Plan Your Holding → Freight for the household**, choose a recruited miner, material, direction, marked mine stockpile and chest on the selected floor. The same Dwarf carries up to 32 units through excavated passages, mine stairs or ladders, the fitted cellar stair and built household floors. Use **U** in the mine away from the entrance and wheelbarrow to mark a stockpile.

Loads move only at the actual stores. Household goods stay in their chest until withdrawn for crafting. Miners use their usual provisions, finish carried loads before resting, and retain their ordinary mine assignments. Use **Finish load & stop**, **Return carried load & stop**, or **Resume route**. If a stair is blocked or a chest removed, the load stays with its carrier. Position, floor, order and cargo survive saving and reloading.

Carriers display an existing timber crate in household and mine views. Wheelbarrows stay in the mine; this update adds porter routes, not cart-sized household stairs. Handworks raw-load pallets, automated excavation and property-story integration remain separate.

Extract the complete Windows ZIP into a fresh folder and keep FirstFortune.exe beside FirstFortune.pck. Preserve older builds and saves. See CURRENT_STATUS.md for the verified release and source/package evidence. [Previous notes](history/RELEASE_NOTES-before-freight162.md).
