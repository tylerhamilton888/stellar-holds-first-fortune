# The old cutters’ bay — workplace 98 preview

A seeded, abandoned stonecutters’ bay is added only when a new hold mine is generated. Existing excavations are never rewritten. The collapsed supply route, worn fitted floor, surviving rack and breaker, damaged hearth bed and Slatehand tally describe one connected place rather than an unrelated reward room. The works catalogue and concealed equipment do not reveal it before discovery.

Restoration uses ordinary mining and LivingWorks furnishing: clear the passage, install a Forge hearth & anvil over the old bed through O → Furnish, then press E with one coal. Fuel lasts 1,800 active seconds. The rack and breaker are real Handworks records. Odrin Slatehand walks from the entrance when there is warmth and a viable route, draws actual raw stock, and leaves the original yield as collectable bench cargo. He takes a calendar midday break, steps away for player work, and heads home outside his shift or when the hearth goes cold. A blocked path stops him without teleporting. Reopening and relighting let the same resident return.

Position, discovery, heat, unfinished work, materials and Odrin’s cumulative work history persist. On returning from elsewhere, supplied work can advance for time within his working hours and remaining heat, bounded by actual rack input and output capacity; it cannot create free materials. This is one local resident/place prototype, not a universal offscreen population simulation. His dialogue responds to stock, access, warmth and completed work. Original Dwarf and furnishing art is reused in overhead and exploration views.
