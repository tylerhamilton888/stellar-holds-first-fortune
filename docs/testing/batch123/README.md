`batch-poses.gif` shows the final five characters using actual Godot textures and metadata, at 1.5x gameplay stature. It steps all four pose keys at the walking cadence for comparison. Drinking and work retain their existing, separate timing in the game.

The five `*-gait.png` boards show the complete resting and four-direction walking sets at native size and 2x. The six `*-actions.png` boards show drinking, hammering and front/side/back mining where those activities are reachable; Yrsa's original drinking poses remain beside her new work poses. Odrin only uses hammering.

Live captures cover hiring seats, supplied mining/workshop work, and Odrin's existing workplace. Workshop test snapshots can retain notification labels from accelerated simulation; those labels are not part of the character art. Source and exact-package results, asset hashes and publication provenance are in `acceptance.json`, `source-results.json` and `pack-results.json`.

Visual review covered alternating physical feet, linked wrists and grips, two-point pick heads, beard and costume continuity, alpha edges, boot baselines and fixed body scale through raised-tool poses. The rejected opaque Osvik attempts are documented in `docs/art/batch123/README.md`. Technical test results do not replace the owner's visual feedback.
