# Navigation94 — optional mine perspective and explored-floor map

The new HoldMineView94 is presentation only. It uses a SubViewport, original whole-character frames, original prop artwork and nearby tile geometry; Deepworks remains the authority for movement, collision, digging and saved layers. No 3D physics bodies or duplicate world save are added. HoldDepthNavigation94 owns transient map browsing and BFS guidance through known cells and actual stair links. View preference uses user://view_settings94.cfg, outside fortunes.

Controls: F6 view; brackets quarter-turn; Z paused depth map; Page Up/Down browsed floor; click explored ground for route; E/Shift+E actual stairs. Placement modes automatically use overhead and restore exploration afterward. X still renders the original geological survey. Combat aim projects at body height while mining uses the ground plane. Original workstations retain texture ownership across viewport draws.

Native focused source evidence: test-results/navigation94-highlight, 31 assertions, exit zero and no script errors. Covers unchanged position/inventory across toggles, no map mutation/reveal, real cross-floor links, cart/ladder restriction, camera-relative movement and zero-energy dodge, mouse-selected mining contact, mouse-selected combat direction and damage at zero energy, construction fallback, survey, menu pause, preference/save reload and normal-fortune isolation of the developer preset.

Visual review includes the original character in both camera orientations, enlarged stair labels, workbench and raw load, attack cone, survey and parchment map. The workstation texture lifetime defect and floating cutaway mining highlight were repaired before acceptance. Final validation: 197 local regression assertions; 31 final focused navigation assertions after the cutaway-highlight correction; all 37 hosted source cases plus people-preview; all 27 exact-export cases / 1,924 assertions. See navigation94/delivery.json for commits, runs, checksums and exact image comparisons. The runtime PR #59 is merged and v0.84.0 is published. Screenshots in navigation94/ are gameplay captures, not mockups.

Scope and remaining checks: mine-only prototype, one floor at a time, quarter-turn camera and marked E stair transitions. Routes guide manual movement and can be replanned after excavation/construction; they do not auto-walk. Construction material finishes and every advanced cargo/work overlay are not claimed at parity with overhead. Native Linux software rendering is verified; Windows device performance/audio and subjective camera comfort require owner playtesting.

Local frame-time probe: the same small two-floor fixture measured overhead idle 27.13 ms mean, exploration idle 20.49 ms, moving 20.81 ms and combat 23.05 ms (combat p95 30.89 ms), with Mesa llvmpipe at 1280×720. Each sample excludes 30 warmup frames and measures 120 rendered frames. These are limited software-rendering observations, not Windows hardware guarantees; see navigation94/performance.txt.
