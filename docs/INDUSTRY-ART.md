Generate one powered industrial upgrade of the left forge building, same front-facing side-scroller perspective, same heavy gray stone arch and foundation, slate roof and bronze carved dwarf details. Entire single building centered, base horizontal, no ground scenery, no people, no letters, no extra objects, all exterior empty pixels transparent. Similar proportions to reference left building, generous transparent margin.

## Sealed engineering bay prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one transparent 2D dwarf sci-fi engineering workshop facade for side-scrolling game. Reference image existing dwarf architecture. Match left forge silhouette and front-facing view, horizontal heavy gray stone foundation, slate pitched roof, carved bronze knotwork. Late technological upgrade, recognize same stone arch building but fitted with large precisely machined dark steel pressure door open to an amber-lit assembly bench, enclosed turbine cylinder on left, ceramic insulated power bus above arch, industrial ventilation and coolant pipes on right, narrow blue instrument windows, small radar receiver on roof. Practical starship engineering bay; dwarven heritage stone and bronze framing integrates serious steel industrial equipment. No steampunk gears everywhere, no excessive glowing runes. Rich crisp painted 2D game art readable at 220px wide, no photorealism. Full building including chimney tips within frame, generous transparent margin all sides. Flat facade viewed from front, not isometric. No people, text, ground, scenery. One building only. Genuine transparency.

Supported gate frames, animated hoist cables and the fixed-size slagcaster are drawn in game code. Workshop press/assembly indicators animate only while a production job is active. The short thermal pulse sound is original synthesized audio (190ms, peak -6.9dBFS, played at an additional -9dB through Effects); existing supplied music and mining recordings are retained.
