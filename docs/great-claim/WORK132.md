# Useful hands: Stonebridge work and contribution

The opening keeps its worker-to-Thane story. Short optional lesson chains teach ore processing/smelting/tools, timber/hauling/furnishing, surveying/safe descent/crystal, and food production/cooking. Actual completed work supplies proof; purchases, gifts, refunds and waiting do not. Steps can be tracked in J and reported at the relevant Stonebridge desk. Earlier work in this version counts even before selecting a lesson; rewards are once-only.

The contest has two compatible contribution routes. Existing civic projects still create their visible town improvements. Three trade seals, worth 40 marks each, accept player-produced supplies. Any combination of mining, timber, metalworking and provisions is valid, including three deliveries in one trade. Each seal consumes both carried materials and its recorded work credit. The shared three-seal limit prevents collecting a fresh 120 marks from every profession. Typical deliveries have similar base material value (48–60 crowns) to the small civic projects (40–52); production chains and travel also matter. No equal human completion time is claimed: test active time for specialist and mixed routes during owner playtesting.

A paid introductory shift plus any two civic projects/trade seals earns the household plot. Registering the home still gives 60 marks. Three trade seals plus the home reach the visible 180-mark rescue target without requiring unrelated disciplines. Existing first-eight work credits remain a small bonus. Town projects, including the storehouse, remain another route. Authentication freezes the existing contest record; lesson rewards remain available afterward, but new contest trade seals close. A high work score remains intact and the public skill challenge still follows; no rival score chases the player's score.

Save data is additive under surface.work132. Historical scores, completed projects, houses, inventories, relationships and closed-round ancestry records remain authoritative. Existing saves begin production accounting when first loaded into this feature; purchased historical inventory is not guessed to be player-made.

Scope: clearer journal hierarchy, a readable work/contribution board, Stonebridge-local report actions and steel-pick crafting, production proof hooks, save/replay/locality tests and native/exact-pack review. No new story chapter, profession lock, mandatory day wait, calendar deadline or art replacement.
