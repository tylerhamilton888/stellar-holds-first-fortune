# Romance cast direction

Owner direction, 2026-11-01: every romance candidate must be conventionally attractive for their respective gender and belong visually and personally to The Stellar Holds.

## Visual acceptance

All candidates are clearly adult Dwarfs. Men are handsome and masculine, with powerful compact builds, substantial brows and full, well-kept beards covering chin and jaw. Women are pretty and distinctly feminine, with appealing expressive faces, flattering hair and clothing, sturdy adult Dwarven proportions and no beards. Vary faces, silhouettes, hair, coloring and temperament while maintaining this standard for every candidate.

Use the established warm, hand-painted whole-character style: readable contours, natural hair pigments, leather, wool, restrained metalwork and practical traditional fantasy clothing. Preserve character identity between portraits, world sprites and every action. Clothing should suit the person's occupation and flatter their build. Source violet remains a technical hair mask, never their intended natural hair color. Review the actual recolored portrait and animated actor together at gameplay scale before acceptance.

## Story-character art budget

Owner clarification: story characters can receive bespoke art that would be impractical for the background population. Major romance candidates should have individually recognizable faces, silhouettes, hairstyles, signature garments and accessories, dedicated portraits and expression sets. Add character-specific gestures and important scene animations where they communicate personality. A palette swap of a common dwarf is a baseline, not the final story-character target.

Keep the shared movement/system foundation where useful, but author complete character frames when their unique silhouette requires it. Match those differences in world sprites, portraits and story scenes. Do not spend the same bespoke production effort on ordinary background workers: they can use the existing modular identities, shared motion and palette variations. Artistic freedom for the story cast still follows the established scale, materials, Dwarven anatomy and warm illustrated style.

## Verification boundary

Selecting established attractive character families provides a consistent baseline, not six bespoke completed characters. Preserve existing acquaintances rather than silently replacing a saved person. Dedicated faces, clothes, expression animation, marriage and the full frontier roster remain future production work. The implemented courtship scenes currently use text choices and existing portrait families. Record visual review and tests in the current status before publishing a build.
