# Family cottage timber wing

On the original Copper Ridge family plot, stand outside and open the homestead panel with O. Choose an end of the cottage. The timber wing costs 8 wood and 4 stone. Each side has its own painted roof, posts and join. The front steps stay aligned with the original house entrance.

Stand near the bay to fit a sawbench in the suggested position, or use free placement. It costs 5 wood and 2 stone. Move an idle player-owned bench from the same panel when standing near it. Left-click a valid position nearby to place it. Right-click cancels. A move retains the bench's inventory and costs no materials. The timber rack remains a separate working station.

Remove the wing to recover its materials. The cottage keeps its repaired exterior and equipment. Room edits and upper floors use their existing tile renderer. This first trial covers one wing at a time on the original cottage. Construction scaffolding for this particular wing is still open.

The native comparison uses the same camera on both sides. The west example places the saw at the entrance to the low bay. The east example moves it into the grass, brings the timber rack alongside and leaves the west yard available for garden beds. The ripe crops are staged for the comparison.

To reproduce, import the project in Godot 4.4.1. Run tests/timber_wing.gd as a SceneTree script against the project with a writable output directory and either west or east after the -- separator. Use a dedicated user data directory for tests. Both runs exercise actual purchases, worker access, blocked placements, save/load and refunds. The browser comparison displays native captures and a sample of positions checked by Godot on that claim.
