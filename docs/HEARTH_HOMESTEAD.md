# Hearth & Homestead — 0.70.0

A relaxed day connects existing gardens, fishing, the production queue, furniture and mastery. No spoilage, mandatory sleep, daily watering, timed favors or exclusive professions.

## Play loop

Craft the existing field kit, select a crop on the field board, and place a garden on your claim (or use leased Greenhearth beds). E loosens soil, plants the selected seed, and waters once. Optional tending advances growth by 15%; mature crops wait indefinitely and return one seed. Turnips make provisions/hotpot, carrots accompany fish, barley makes bread and provides a future brewing grain, and shade-loving hearthcaps make hotpot. This milestone does not implement brewing or underground farm placement.

Stonebridge favors trout; the slow eastern Greenhearth river favors perch; the southern claim tributary favors char. E casts, the float and sound announce a bite, and holding E reels. Bites wait without penalty. You may cast along accessible stretches of either Stonebridge bank, the southern claim bank or eastern Greenhearth bank. The new river reaches follow existing zone boundaries and never replace saved buildings or gardens. Their water reuses the approved terrain atlas; the initially generated isolated pool was rejected after owner feedback and is not shipped.

Prepare food beside your home hearth/counter or a public kitchen. Recipes reuse inventory and the production queue. Ordinary trout/turnip provisions are preserved. Serve new meals beside a table or counter at home: root hotpot improves workshop speed 5%, bread packs five provisions, chowder improves selling terms, and char with roots improves workshop speed 10%. Benefits expire by world time, never consume additional food, and workshop bonuses do not stack.

Six comfort categories count once each: rest, warmth, light, storage, personal character and a table with a chair within 110 units. Existing fixed furnishings count. Each category adds 30 seconds to supper; four categories add one provision per served meal. Paid crafting awards Woodworking; moving/recovering furniture does not. Hearthkeeping research adds two minutes to supper and carved pantry/family-table plans. Ordinary shelves and tables provide identical comfort. Existing architectural materials, multistory construction and cottage improvement remain available.

Three optional mastery techniques: Gentle tending (bonus crop after tending), Reading the ripples (earlier bite), A welcoming table (longer supper at a dining corner). No recipe or crop requires specialization. Orla buys and sells all seeds, ingredients and meals.

Hedrik's optional welcoming-table favor asks for either hotpot or chowder after serving a meal at home with three comforts. Bought food qualifies. Visit the invitation beside the tavern counter; receive 25 crowns, barley seeds and Hearthkeeping research once. This preserves his anniversary story and household routine.
