# 0.125.0 · Stonebridge

Stand for Thane of Stonebridge. Meet Skarrin at the bunkhouse, take up Borin’s forge work, or ask Ivar to walk you to the holding your parents bought. The cottage, workshops, underground depths and town buildings now ship together in the normal campaign.

- Press **I** to find a supply, filter by category, inspect its use, or assign it to a quick-bar slot. Switch from carried items to the catalogue to see materials you have yet to collect.
- Seeds, stone blocks, pick components and ingots have individually painted icons. Recipes show their names, ingredients and shortages. The gear page displays the weapons you own.
- The campaign recognizes saves from the previous Stonebridge Campaign launcher. The Windows build starts from its own executable and data pack.
