# Stonebridge playtest

Use the [latest published Windows build](https://github.com/tylerhamilton888/stellar-holds-first-fortune/releases/latest) for these sessions.

- Give a first-time player an uninterrupted stretch from New Game through a first entry in the Ledger. Watch whether they find the south door, recognize the forge on the main street and inspect the hearth unaided. Ask what relighting it will do for their standing against Skarrin.
- Watch a new player find an ingredient, tell a missing item from a carried one, and pin a supply without prompting.
- Test the house route, moving a workshop, and the progression from a first trench to a multi-level excavation from a normal campaign save.

Previous work and unresolved playtest ideas are preserved in archive/BEFORE_STONEBRIDGE_MENUS.md.

Approach the town Ledger via each path, then deliberately take the southeast fork to Pinewood. Compare empty-handed and pick-equipped footfalls along the street and use the Ledger tab to return to the same project from the journal.
