# First independent outpost — acceptance target

The opening commissions teach useful work and fund a choice of mountain. Thereafter the player chooses which seams to follow, which freight contracts to fill, which dwarf to recruit, and how to allocate production toward their first ship. The board guides without mandating a sequence of personality events.

## Player check
1. Reach a charter without outside instruction; keep materials for the next expedition.
2. Name the outpost; build a workshop and recruit two different dwarves at the Copper Kettle.
3. Compare their dialogue, assign a miner and smith, and verify a visible benefit from each.
4. Complete an optional personal project and read its chronicle entry.
5. Finish the freight beacon, recruit remotely, and assign an engineer.
6. Work toward the keel, hull and drive; build with the hammer at the launchyard.
7. Save/reload, travel away and back, and verify personalities, assignments and terrain persist.

## Deliberate boundaries
The twelve named local/recruit characters remain authored anchors; secondary traits and projects vary by seed. The current chronicle reports real events, but does not yet simulate friendships, rivalries, families, migration or a procedural galaxy. New text is not voiced; the existing supplied Ivar speech remains the voiced opening.

## Naming
Oakenshield and Farsight are retained only as migration lookup keys. New generated surnames include Fellcopper, Slateweld, Coilwarden, Mossrivet, Flintgauge, Cairnweft and Brasshaven. No legal clearance claim is made.
