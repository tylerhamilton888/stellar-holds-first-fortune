# Art direction — character models are the gold standard
User direction, 2026-10-18: the existing in-world Dwarf character models are the authoritative visual reference for in-world items and environments. Portraits and cutscenes may use a different treatment.

Use the current directional Dwarf artwork (`assets/directional-v47/male.png`, `female.png`) and its in-game rendering as the primary comparison. Match its chunky proportions, readable silhouettes, crisp illustrated contours, stylized volume, restrained material detail and warm, expressive character. Existing props are useful supporting references only; a newer or more detailed prop does not supersede the characters as the standard.

Tools, carried resources, stockpiles, workstations, buildings and terrain should look at home beside that Dwarf at actual gameplay scale. Avoid photorealistic surface noise, conflicting pixel density, overly glossy metal, tiny illegible decoration or plain stand-in geometry. Consistent art does not require identical colors: wood, stone, iron and copper retain distinct readable material identities.

Use one recognizable material representation through ground, hands, transport, stockpile and processing states. Any changes in shape or finish must communicate actual processing or damage. Equipment must belong to a real activity or stored load; avoid orphaned, floating or duplicate held props. Animation must preserve model identity, scale, facing and believable grips/contact.

Validate new artwork and animation beside the Dwarf in the real game at the normal viewport, including movement, contact, resting, empty and loaded states. Inspect silhouette/outline, relative size, lighting direction, shadow depth, detail density and UI legibility. A standalone asset preview or successful parse is not sufficient visual acceptance.

Portraits, romance slides and other story/cutscene images may be more painterly, exaggerated or cinematic while preserving character identity and their own internal continuity. Their style should not drift back into normal gameplay sprites.
