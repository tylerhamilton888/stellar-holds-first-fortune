# Start in Stonebridge

Extract the complete Windows ZIP into a folder and open the game executable. Choose New Game, an empty slot and a dwarf. Use Load Game for an existing Stonebridge Campaign save.

Use WASD to walk and E to interact. Your first quest, **A hearth gone cold**, starts as soon as you finish or skip the welcome. Walk out through the south door of the bunkhouse. Head east along the street to the marked communal forge, walk inside, and press E at the cold hearth. Choose **Inspect the cold hearth**. Your journal now tracks the coal Borin needs. You can also ask Ivar to show you the Rise.

[The first quest at the bunkhouse door](stonebridge-playtest/quest-exit.webp)

Follow the west fork for the Ledger and the southeast fork for Pinewood. Press I for the pack, J for the journal or M for the Ledger. From the book in town, E opens the delivery page. When testing inventory, search for a material, switch between carried items and the catalogue, and pin a supply to a quick-bar slot. Escape closes the page.

For deeper testing, check building placement at the cottage, walking up and down the lodge stairs, mining beneath the holding and returning through the same entrance. Reload midway through the introduction or after making a change at home.

To check the walking animation, cross a clear stretch of road sideways and then towards the camera. Compare the starting pick with empty hands. [Before and after at game speed](stonebridge-playtest/walk-0.125.4.mp4).
