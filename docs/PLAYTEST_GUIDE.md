# Current Windows playtest guide

The verified version and release evidence are recorded in [CURRENT_STATUS.md](CURRENT_STATUS.md). This guide covers the current chapter; older controls and version-specific trials are kept in [historical playtests](history/PLAYTEST_GUIDE-before-doc-consolidation-2026-11-10.md).

## Install and choose a save

1. Open the [latest published release](https://github.com/tylerhamilton888/stellar-holds-first-fortune/releases/latest) and download **Stellar-Holds-First-Fortune-<version>-Windows.zip**. Do not choose GitHub's automatic source ZIP or tarball.
2. Extract the whole ZIP into a fresh folder. Keep **FirstFortune.exe** and **FirstFortune.pck** together with the supplied supporting files. Do not run from inside the ZIP or combine files from different builds.
3. Keep the previous build and saves. Use **New Game** and an empty fortune slot for a fresh opening test, or **Load Game** to check an existing fortune. Older non-frontier fortunes retain their earlier route.

## First hour

Meet Ivar in the bunkhouse, visit your upstairs room, and follow the journal (**J**) to the first paid stone load at Old Workings. Use the in-world prompts for the current work step. Select the pick with **1**, stand still and hold the left mouse button to excavate. **E** lifts a freed load or stocks the western loan rack; hold **E** at the western stonebreaker to work. New output enters your pack; **E** collects older bench output. The eastern crew's stock belongs to the hold.

Return to Ivar's shift book. Find the enormous outdoor Ledger on its gilded stone lectern between two braziers, east of the bunkhouse. Stand at its front and press **E**. **M** reviews the same Ledger remotely; deliveries require the physical book or original indoor records desk and goods in your pack. Partial deliveries are saved.

After your first pay, the journal offers room furnishing, a visit to Borin first, or an alternate trade. Furnishing is optional. When following the forge, its next-step guidance updates after inspection, coal processing and partial deliveries; the HUD follows the same lead.

Follow the Cold Forge project's actual requirements, make your contributions, and return for its first firing. Choose a trade lesson and an optional collection that interests you; all three collections are not required. Follow current Ledger and journal requirements toward your home.

Record any moment where you cannot tell what to do, where to go, which object to use or why an action is blocked. Note repeated trips, confusing prompts, overly long explanations and whether your first pay and forge restoration feel rewarding.

## Surface room blueprints (0.107.0)

On your chartered holding, open the Building Book (**O**) and choose **Plan Your Holding**. Drag a rectangle; click a plan or use the selector to inspect it. Scroll the map to zoom, middle-drag to pan, or use the position/overview controls below. New Room starts another rectangle; redraw only unstarted plans. Bedroom designation suggests oak flooring; equipment still supplies every actual function.

Add a replacement doorway before removing its old planned doorway. Built walls and doors must be recovered through Fine Construction before changing their openings. Build within reach spends carried materials on up to 32 nearby steps; walk around the room and repeat. The combined estimate counts shared pieces once. Optional finishing choices and research requirements are below the map.

Plan a hall, bedroom, kitchen, workshop and storehouse. Furnish and use them; try a bed and cooking hearth in an undesignated room. Stock a chest, renovate, save and reload. Confirm contents, finishes and unfinished plans remain. Existing manual stairs should still travel to supported upper floors. Removing a plan must leave every physical item intact.

## Underground excavation plans (0.108.0)

In the mine, press **O**, choose **Plan Your Holding** or the **Plans** tab, and drag a chamber or corridor. Plans can include solid stone; labels are optional. Blue guides are planned, amber means excavation has begun, and green means the floor is cleared. Mine normally; the plan spends nothing and grants no ore. Field-dress or haul the resulting chunks using the existing cargo tools.

Once a chamber is cleared and connected, choose **Prepare / furnish**. Use existing furnishings and workstations/storage. Choose floor and natural-wall finishes lower in the book, review the quote, then use **Finish within reach** while moving around the room. Research and actual carried supplies still apply. Removing a guide must leave the excavation, room, furnishings and stored materials intact.

Stair guides apply only to the original five depths. Click **Plan stairs**, select the upper location, and excavate both five-tile landings using existing access to reach below. Stand on the upper guide and choose **Fit stairs here**. Test **E** down and **Shift+E** up, then save/reload. Lateral mineworks retain their adits; surface-to-underground cellar connections are covered below.

## Connected cellar (0.109.0)

On the claim ground floor, use **O → Plan Your Holding → Plan cellar stair here** while standing at the intended landing. Note its tile coordinates. The cellar is the existing claim mine directly beneath the grounds. Build five clear floor tiles at the upper landing; use the existing mine entrance to reach below and mine the five lower tiles plus a connected approach. Return above and fit for 4 timber / 2 stone.

Use **Shift+E** at either landing. Take carried supplies down, furnish a room and stock a materials pallet, then save/reload on both sides. Check that the home, mine plans, furnishings and storage contents survive. Try a blocked landing and a hitched cart; travel should be refused with a reason. Unbuilt guides can be removed, but built stair landings stay protected. Recruited miners with an assigned household berth can cross this connection; carts must remain parked.

## Upper-floor blueprints (0.110.0)

In **O → Plan Your Holding**, select an upper floor and draw a room over your ground plan. Switching floors must not move you or spend resources. An unsupported plan should say **Needs supports**; after building below, it should say **Needs stairs** until a built flight reaches it.

Choose the lower planning floor, stand at an interior landing and use **Plan upward stair at my X/Y**. Fit it locally for **4 timber / 2 stone**. E climbs and Shift+E descends. Select the visited floor before **Build within reach**; walk around inside the room to reach its far walls. Reused roof decks must not charge another floor cost.

Make a cellar workshop/storage area, a ground kitchen/hall and an upstairs bedroom. Use their furnishings and storage, renovate upstairs, save and reload. Confirm the lower floor and stored items remain intact. Removing an upper plan must preserve the physical room. Existing manual stairs remain supported; carts stay parked, and automated freight across the household boundary is still future work.

## Crew berths — 0.111.1

On your own claim, recruit and assign a miner, furnish a real bed, and connect it to the mine with clear constructed floors, household stairs and a fitted cellar stair. In **O → Plan Your Holding → A bed for your crew**, select the bed's floor and choose **Assign selected bed**. Room designation is optional. Keep provisions available.

During the existing crew rest period, watch the miner walk home, rest at the bedside and return to the original mine assignment. Save and reload during the commute and upstairs: position and berth should survive without a duplicate worker. Try an obstructed route and then clear it; the worker should wait and resume, never cross the obstruction or gain rest remotely. Release the berth to return the worker to normal duties. Existing saves and workers at other worksites should retain their worksite behavior.

Carts still stay parked at household stairs. This release adds worker movement, not automated storage transfers. Check normal-speed Windows performance and readability during ordinary play.
