# Start in Stonebridge

Extract the complete Windows ZIP into a folder and open the game executable. Choose New Game, an empty slot and a dwarf. Use Load Game for an existing Stonebridge Campaign save.

Use WASD to walk and E to interact. Try Ivar’s walk to the Rise, Borin’s cold forge or the Ledger Shrine across the street. Follow the west fork for the Ledger and the southeast fork for Pinewood. Press I for the pack, J for the journal or M for the Ledger. From the book in town, E opens the delivery page. When testing inventory, search for a material, switch between carried items and the catalogue, and pin a supply to a quick-bar slot. Escape closes the page.

For deeper testing, check building placement at the cottage, walking up and down the lodge stairs, mining beneath the holding and returning through the same entrance. Reload midway through the introduction or after making a change at home.
