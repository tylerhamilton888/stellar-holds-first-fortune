# Try 0.46.0 — your hold, trail and home
Extract the whole Windows ZIP into a new folder and run PLAY-WINDOWS.bat. Keep your earlier build. Old saves receive a separate backup before the new location data is added.

1. Start or load normally. Walk with WASD/arrows; press E near a doorway or person. Follow J introductions through real buildings. Check the Lord on his throne and Borin working in the forge.
2. Use the stairs and lift between the three hold wards. G gives directions. Walk out through the King's Gate to the High Trail; follow its north branch to the public mine, south branch to Pinewood and east branch toward your claim.
3. In the mine, excavate, create a real lower passage and return along your route. Existing mining, masonry, containers and workers retain their controls. In Pinewood equip the axe and hold LMB at a reachable trunk/log; check the falling tree and gathering rhythm.
4. Acquire your claim through the existing charter progression, or visit your existing claim. Your cottage and first chest are ready. Enter the home, use E at the bed, then E at the chest. Store timber and compare Pack/Chest counts. Close it, walk away and reopen; items should remain there.
5. O in your home/yard opens the homestead board. Place another chest (4 timber), or table/lamp (3 timber + 2 stone); choose clear nearby floor. Right-click cancels. Improve the cottage for 12 timber + 8 stone. Costs use carried supplies, so withdraw what you need first.
6. Return to a shop/forge and sell/craft from your pack. Save inside your home, reload and check location, decorations, chest contents and existing mine progress. In an established save, check your crew and masonry handover still work.

Aim to judge whether doors are easy to find, movement feels comfortable, the character fits the rooms, and menus stay readable while scrolling. The new home is a starter cottage with an upgrade and placeable furnishings; freeform house construction is not included yet. Eight-direction walking currently retains the original left/right-facing Dwarf animation style. The sparring practice bay still uses its older mechanics.

# Try 0.45.0 — a focused 20–30 minute pass
Extract the entire Windows ZIP to a new folder and run PLAY-WINDOWS.bat. Keep your prior build and save backup. Expect the existing game, settlement and progression, with five persistent top-down mine depths and the Living Works. A fresh character still follows the opening; advanced rooms/crew require supplies and recruitment.

Start the new lesson with **O → Masonry** underground (also linked from J). Chisel ordinary stone with held LMB; mark a 4×4 or larger room on Rooms; place the mason's bench (3 timber + 2 stored stone). Cache the block on walkable floor within three tiles, then choose Dress blocks and hold LMB over the bench. Shift+U loads finished stone; U at the entrance delivers it. Lay a dressed floor tile from Masonry and complete Brann's lesson. Later, in your claimed mountain, add the bench to a functional workshop and assign a recruited Dwarf. Watch him fetch a block, dress it and return output. Save during a carrying phase and verify conservation after reload.

Ordinary stone remains usable for all previous basic recipes. Optional block reduction makes four ordinary stone; dressing makes two dressed stone, or three with Fine Masonry. The lesson credits previous work. No repeated bulk-production requirement or stamina bar.

1. **Dig and explore.** Travel from the settlement to Old Workings or your claim. WASD/arrows move; hold LMB with pick [1]. Try diagonal movement, ordinary stone, ore and a cavern. Does impact match the target? Are minerals, cracks and open floor easy to read? X previews the layer below.
2. **Prove a route.** V offers carved stairs or a ladder costing 2 stored timber. E descends; Shift+E ascends at the same landing. Return to satisfy Ivar's route lesson, then claim it normally. Park a wheelbarrow before using a ladder; upgrade from above to stairs for freight. Follow a vein into a second depth.
3. **Bring useful work home.** H manages hauling; C takes/parks a cart; U delivers at the entrance or transfers nearby cargo. Return to settlement and craft or sell delivered materials. Field cargo is not yet stored inventory.
4. **Make a room worth using.** With supplies, clear 4×4 to 9×9 floor. O designates a workshop; bench + forge/anvil + lantern make it functional. Place nearby with LMB; finish surfaces by holding. Right mouse cancels. Look for material feedback and quality increases. E at the bench opens crafting. At your claimed mountain, assign a recruited Dwarf through O; observe him reach the room, then release him to mining. Bedrooms need bed + lantern.
5. **Check the stories.** Visit Lord Eirik through the existing audience route. After the opening unlocks side stories, visit Hedrik and Orla locally to help with both gifts. The second recipient invites you to the anniversary. Watch expressions, prop consistency and text readability; Next/Space advances, Escape skips safely. Replays should not duplicate rewards. Try the permanent tea table afterward.
6. **Save and return.** F5, quit and reload. Confirm excavations, depth, passages, cargo, room furnishings and assignments. Visit Pinewood and check its independent timber loop still behaves normally.

Most useful feedback: where work felt satisfying or tedious; unclear controls; menus obscuring action; awkward animation/contact; confusing quest instructions. For bugs, include version, screenshot, last few actions and expected versus actual behavior.

Known scope: five depths, workshops/bedrooms, existing automation. No rails, brewing simulation or expanded craft skill system yet. Masonry delegation is implemented; other specialist professions still use their existing systems. Tested with Linux Godot 4.4.1 including exported game pack; the bundled Windows runtime needs your platform check. Audio events are tested, speaker balance needs listening.
