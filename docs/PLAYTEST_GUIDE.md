# Playtest 0.54.0 — Reference-led Folk
Extract the entire Windows ZIP, keep FirstFortune.exe and FirstFortune.pck together, and launch FirstFortune.exe. Continue uses the same save locations. The first pre-.54 load preserves an exact backup. This release changes character presentation, scale, mining weight and Keldrath rendering; it is not yet the larger connected-world expansion.

1. **Identity and motion:** In a new fortune or appearance editor, try all seven male and seven female designs, colors, clasp/pendant and marks. Compare bust and live sprite. Walk in every direction, turn, stop and walk against a wall. Hair, face and clothing should stay consistent. Ivar's walk should lead naturally to the quarters; check chest and bed in either order.
2. **Hold scale and navigation:** Visit the exchange, tavern, forge and Lord. Use the doors, both stairways, lift and High Trail bridge. Compare Dwarf height to a doorway. Watch Keldrath's first visit versus return visits and report noticeable hitches with your hardware and area. A short pose-preparation screen may appear on first entry.
3. **Mining weight:** Enter Old Workings from the trail. Hold LMB on a reachable exposed face in each cardinal direction. Ordinary slate takes two starting-pick contacts and keeps the first fracture; coal two, iron three. You should stay planted until you move yourself. Holding at the cleared face must not drag you through the next wall. Check the pick tip, crack, sound and debris together. Harder geology and better tools retain progression.
4. **Depth and discoveries:** Follow a real vein, carve a stair, descend and return at the same location. Survey the layer below and find a cavern/geode. Save with a partly damaged rock, reload, and check excavation, depth, discoveries and cargo. Existing miners and machinery should still work.
5. **Tangible labor:** In Pinewood hold axe [3] on a trunk, then E to lift its raw log. Mine a raw stone chunk. Shift+B crafts/placeable stations, stockpiles, pallets and transport. E deposits, Shift+E withdraws; hold E at a supplied station to work. The same material should appear on shoulders, stockpile and bench. Check output conservation, then build, craft or sell it. Strong Shoulders follows eight productive masonry/forestry credits; try two stones, two logs and mixed cargo.
6. **Transport and combat:** C takes/parks cart handles. Travel the gate, mine door and carved stairs with a load, then reload. Wagons park outside interiors; ladders/house stairs require parking. Test Borin's existing practice: mouse aim, held LMB attack, RMB guard/parry, Q dodge. Menus pause combat; defeats and enemy wounds persist.

The 1–0 bar shows the held tool/item and quantities; I opens the pack, G directions, J quests. The mine depth label should stay unobstructed. Fishing, gardening, personal chests and crafting/automation remain available through the existing lessons.

Please report the area/depth, male/female design, selected tool, input and screenshot/video for a visual or contact problem. Windows launch, audio and your judgement of feel are the remaining acceptance checks. Native tests here use Linux Godot 4.4.1; the larger world, remaining older interior props and further directional/armor art are tracked follow-ups.
