# 0.55.0 — Try one promising mining site
Extract the complete Windows ZIP and run FirstFortune.exe beside FirstFortune.pck. Continue keeps your existing progress; the first upgraded load makes a separate `.pre-mining-55-*.bak`. Keep that file if returning to an older build.

Aim for a **10–15 minute visit**, with a few short pauses to judge the feel:

1. Walk from Keldrath through the High Trail into the existing mine. Pick an exposed rock or ore face. Hold LMB: look for anticipation, contact, progressively deeper fractures and a substantial freed load. Move with WASD mid-stroke; the Dwarf should respond immediately.
2. Try two approaches. Keep scoring with the pick [1], or use the hammer [4] once the face says **split-ready**. An exposed corner should be easier than a solid heading. Holding at an opened face must not pull you forward through the next wall.
3. Recover a load. E lifts, Q sets down. Away from equipment, hold **Shift+E** beside a load or while carrying it to dress the whole yield in three blows. The bucket delivers at the mine exit. No station is required for a first coal commission.
4. Establish logistics with **Shift+B**: a raw stockpile and stonebreaker, then a cart when you can afford one. E at the rack or parked cart gathers nearby loose loads. Keep the rack within 150 units of the breaker; hold E to process its batch. Shift+E collects output. C takes/parks cart handles. A nearby pallet can receive bench output.
5. Follow a promising mineral band. **Shift+LMB** recovers it carefully; a nearby work lamp [V] improves recovery. **X** previews the lower layer. Carve a stair [V], descend with E and return with Shift+E at the same location. The V ledger should recognize a formation you work on multiple depths.
6. If you have Stoneworking, place an existing masonry pillar [O] near the face. With steel equipment or master Miner skill, a hammer can free a second deeply fractured, exposed face. Supports are useful, never compulsory for basic digging.
7. Return home, craft or sell some processed material, save, reload, and revisit. Check the same tunnel, damage, raw reserves, equipment and passages. If you have crew, assign them [T] and verify their routes. Take a brief Pinewood trip to confirm its separate timber loop.

Miner practice: 12 freed faces gives strong shoulders and joint reading; 40 improves section control and adds four cart/wagon loads. Your existing masonry/forestry shoulder upgrade also still works. Powered workshop machinery improves bench processing.

Judge whether your site feels **developed**, not merely cleared: a useful route, visible material reserves, a functioning work corner and a reason to return to a lode. Note anything that still feels too fast, too repetitive or unclear. Windows performance, sound balance and your subjective assessment matter; the automated route does not substitute for them.
