# Current Windows playtest guide

The verified version and release evidence are recorded in [CURRENT_STATUS.md](CURRENT_STATUS.md). This guide covers the current chapter; older controls and version-specific trials are kept in [historical playtests](history/PLAYTEST_GUIDE-before-doc-consolidation-2026-11-10.md).

## Install and choose a save

1. Open the [latest published release](https://github.com/tylerhamilton888/stellar-holds-first-fortune/releases/latest) and download **Stellar-Holds-First-Fortune-<version>-Windows.zip**. Do not choose GitHub's automatic source ZIP or tarball.
2. Extract the whole ZIP into a fresh folder. Keep **FirstFortune.exe** and **FirstFortune.pck** together with the supplied supporting files. Do not run from inside the ZIP or combine files from different builds.
3. Keep the previous build and saves. Use **New Game** and an empty fortune slot for a fresh opening test, or **Load Game** to check an existing fortune. Older non-frontier fortunes retain their earlier route.

## First hour

Meet Ivar in the bunkhouse, visit your upstairs room, and follow the journal (**J**) to the first paid stone load at Old Workings. Use the in-world prompts for the current work step. Select the pick with **1**, stand still and hold the left mouse button to excavate. **E** lifts a freed load or stocks the western loan rack; hold **E** at the western stonebreaker to work. New output enters your pack; **E** collects older bench output. The eastern crew's stock belongs to the hold.

Return to Ivar's shift book. Find the enormous outdoor Ledger on its gilded stone lectern between two braziers, east of the bunkhouse. Stand at its front and press **E**. **M** reviews the same Ledger remotely; deliveries require the physical book or original indoor records desk and goods in your pack. Partial deliveries are saved.

Follow the Cold Forge project's actual requirements, make your contributions, and return for its first firing. Choose a trade lesson and an optional collection that interests you; all three collections are not required. Follow current Ledger and journal requirements toward your home.

Record any moment where you cannot tell what to do, where to go, which object to use or why an action is blocked. Note repeated trips, confusing prompts, overly long explanations and whether your first pay and forge restoration feel rewarding.

## Revisit completed work and people

- Leave and reopen a completed favor or civic project: it should acknowledge completion without requesting another delivery.
- Revisit a restored forge and learned steel research: guidance should reflect saved progress.
- Scroll a conversation, then choose a reply: the new reply should begin visibly at the top.
- Try gifts, favors, friendship errands and an available courtship moment. Costs and commitments should be clear. Waiting messages should distinguish play-time limits, calendar delays and insufficient trust.
- Save, reload and confirm your possessions, work and relationships persist.

Existing forge rooms retain their saved layout and objects; newly created rooms use the compact layouts. Check station approaches, furniture, sound levels and activity.

## Continue the chapter

The main route includes trade foundations and a home, Winter Stores, the survey-crew rescue, North Crossing, ancestry and engineering proof, Keldrath's defense and the Thane homecoming. Follow the current in-game requirements. The contest is a story framework with no rival countdown.

Optional collections, friendship episodes and the three persistent mineworks remain available. Mineworks support named districts and gated exploration; they are not an additional requirement for becoming Thane. Marriage remains future work.

Report the displayed game version, fresh or existing save, location, expected action, actual result and whether it repeats after reloading. Record active play time separately from pauses. Automated acceptance does not establish enjoyment, Windows device performance or audio balance.
