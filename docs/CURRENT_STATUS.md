## Player walking animation

The near and far boots exchange support in an eight-pose cycle with empty hands, the starting pick and the other hotbar equipment. Watch both feet on level ground, especially sideways and towards the camera. The two columns in the [gameplay recording](stonebridge-playtest/walk-0.125.4.mp4) follow the same walking inputs at the same speed. We also replayed all 260 movement samples using the PCK extracted from the Windows candidate.

## Your first quest

Skarrin is your rival in the contest for Thane. Ivar gives you a first town project, **A hearth gone cold**. When the welcome ends, walk out the bunkhouse’s south door, follow the main street east to the communal forge and inspect Borin’s hearth. The journal updates to show how much coal the forge needs. The same guidance appears after skipping the scene or loading an unfinished quest. Ivar can show you Old Stonewright’s Rise whenever you choose.

[First playable moment](stonebridge-playtest/quest-exit.webp) · [Playtest guide](PLAYTEST_GUIDE.md)

Walk north up stairs or south to descend. Approach the Ledger via the west fork south of the main street. Pinewood is down the southeast branch. [Ledger court](stonebridge-playtest/court.webp) · [Northern treeline](stonebridge-playtest/trees.webp) · [Ledger page](stonebridge-playtest/ledger.webp)
