# Original scenery generation — v0.4

Method: built-in image-generation tool. All outputs copied unchanged into the
project. No reference images, existing game assets, or external packs supplied.
The prompts below are the final prompt set used for the shipped panoramas.

## Keldrath hall

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original production background for a side-scrolling 2D dwarf fantasy/science-fiction game, not a mockup. Create a wide 3:1 panoramic pixel-art matte painting of the INSIDE of an enormous inhabited mountain hold. View is straight side-on across a monumental subterranean hall, orthographic side-scroller backdrop, no foreground characters, no UI, no text. Strong architectural mass: towering granite piers, load-bearing arches and huge black basalt doorways, immense carved bearded dwarf kings holding hammers and axes, braided stone beards and Celtic interlace, bronze ribs, heavy masonry, distant galleries receding into blue atmospheric haze. In the left third a gigantic gate with a pale cold light beyond, in the center a great ancestor king statue and warm inhabited stacked windows, in right third a massive deep industrial forge with warm amber furnace light, restrained turquoise aether conduits, an overhead freight rail and bronze lift mechanisms integrated into ancient stonework. Grounded weight and engineering, a prosperous working civilization. Artwork occupies upper 85 percent; bottom 15 percent a quiet dark stone platform edge that can sit behind a separate playable foreground at consistent height. Crisp intentionally clustered pixel art with limited blue-slate, moss-teal, warm sandstone and bronze palette, beautiful rich material detail, strong readable large forms, not blurry or photorealistic, not isometric, not cute toy buildings, not neon cyberpunk, no floating impossible structures. Sense of grandeur, crafted industrial fantasy and subtle advanced technology. Original designs, no copied IP. Image only.

## King's Road valley

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original wide panoramic background plate for a side-scrolling 2D Dwarven fantasy and science-fiction mining game. Create wide 3:1 landscape artwork, crisp intentional pixel-art clusters, rich detailed painterly pixel-art matte, cool slate blue mountains and warm copper sunset. Straight side-on view from a mountain terrace overlooking an enormous alpine valley, NOT isometric. Truly massive stratified mountains with snow peaks, exposed folded geological layers, sheer cliffs and deep pine valleys with rivers and mist. Left third: a gigantic ancient granite dwarf hold gate built into sheer mountain rock, carven bearded king statues flanking tall bronze doors, dwarf interlace patterns and massive stone foundations. Center and right: long vista of three distinct ridges across valley, one dark ruined mining entrance on a forested ridge, one sheer untouched copper-colored mountain, one distant road curling to a mountain pass. Subtle advanced industry: a slender engineered cargo viaduct, bronze freight pylons and restrained turquoise aether power lights integrated into ancient masonry; a tiny distant cargo vessel above the valley for scale, no spaceship filling sky, no neon. Composition upper 85 percent scenery with vast mountains, lower 15 percent quiet dark rock terrace edge suitable behind a separate horizontal playable foreground. No people, no text, no labels, no UI, no logos. Solid gravity and realistic geology but stylized original fantasy pixel art, elegant warm/cool lighting, grandeur, grounded noble Dwarven industrial civilization. Match palette of slate stone, desaturated teal shadows, aged bronze and warm amber light. No copied IP.

## Independent ridge

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original production panoramic pixel-art backdrop for a 2D side-scrolling Dwarven fantasy/sci-fi mining game. Wide 3:1 image, no text no UI no people no foreground buildings. The player's NEW INDEPENDENT MOUNTAIN CLAIM on a remote ridge across a great alpine valley from their old home. Straight side-on scene looking out from a rock shelf; at LEFT and center-left immense sharply stratified rock cliffs rising beyond the top of the frame, promising copper and iron geological bands, an ancient dark disused mine entrance high in the middle distance with broken masonry and subtle dwarven carved lintel. At center a forested ravine with waterfall and a tiny ruin, at right a grand distant mountain hold cut into cliffs far across the valley, its gates only a tiny golden speck, a thin freight viaduct, and immense snow peaks receding into blue atmospheric perspective. The player's claim is wild, quiet, spacious, not a fully built city. Lower 15 percent quiet near-horizontal dark rocky shelf, suitable behind separate interactive foreground. Beautiful intentional pixel-art clusters and restrained small palette, detailed stone, fir forest, warm late-afternoon copper light, desaturated teal shadows and slate blue mountains. Solid believable geology, scale and grandeur, grounded classic Dwarf world with only a few tiny turquoise ancient aether markers in distant ruins as hint of advanced technology. Not isometric, no floating islands, no neon, no photorealism, no foreground spaceship, original designs. This will be layered behind much simpler cartoon pixel characters, so prioritize strong large readable masses over busy texture.
