# 0.111.1 — A home for your crew

Includes the crew berths and connected household routes introduced in 0.111.0. In **O → Plan Your Holding → A bed for your crew**, select a floor and an actual bed, then choose **Assign selected bed**. Recruited miners walk home through built floors and fitted cellar/upper stairs, rest at the bedside, and return to their existing mine assignment. Commutes survive save/reload; blocked paths stop travel safely. Automated freight remains the next separate piece.

This correction keeps the household commute owner confined to the claim site. Legacy miners visiting another worksite retain their original worksite behavior; their saved positions and schedules are not taken over by the new system.

Extract the complete Windows ZIP into a fresh folder and keep FirstFortune.exe beside FirstFortune.pck. Preserve older builds and saves. See CURRENT_STATUS.md for verified delivery. [0.111.0 notes](history/RELEASE_NOTES-before-workers160-legacy.md) retain the complete feature description.
