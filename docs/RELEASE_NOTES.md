# 0.101.0 — Collections of the hold

The Hold’s Ledger now has optional Miner, Smith and Hearthkeeper collections. Deliver a little at a time and earn a useful masonry recipe, a tempered hammer, or a supper-table plan. Hearthkeepers may grow crops or catch fish. Progress is saved, with no deadlines, and collections remain available after the contest.

This build also brings the staged Cold Forge, practical campaign milestones, Winter Stores and North Crossing from source versions 0.98–0.100 into the Windows download. The contest guides authored story acts; the required introductions and later Thane sequence remain in place.

Extract the full ZIP into a fresh folder and run **FirstFortune.exe** beside **FirstFortune.pck**. Keep your previous build and saves. For pacing feedback, select **First visit to the hold** in an empty fortune slot. Follow **J**, review **M**, and use the physical bunkhouse Ledger for deliveries. Try one optional profession alongside the town projects and note unclear objectives, resource bottlenecks and whether each new day offers a useful goal. Windows audio, performance and normal-speed pacing need your playtest.

Playtest the first shift, mining/crafting, earning your room and home, menu clarity, combat feel, audio and performance on your PC. Normal-speed pacing and Windows hardware quality still require human playtesting. Opening-chapter development stops at the Thane homecoming; marriage and district expansion remain future work.

# 0.97.0 — Giants at the stairs

Trolls now tower over Dwarfs at twice their previous height. Their larger physical footprint and new whole painted animations carry into the mines, Keldrath's defense and the King's five-troll encounter. Eirik returns with a much larger trophy head.

Watch each fist. An amber circle marks its committed landing; step or Space-dodge out, prepare for the opposite fist, then attack during the long recovery. Timed parries can interrupt the sequence. Troll health and rewards are unchanged, and saved wounds and cleared encounters remain intact. Combat framing leaves more room for the giants, with the same circular warning in the optional mine perspective.

# 0.96.0 — Useful hands

Choose how you earn your place in Stonebridge. Twelve optional lessons guide ore processing, smelting, tool upgrades, timber, loaded carts, furnishing, surveys, safe descents, crystal recovery, fieldwork and cooking. J shows the immediate step, your chosen lesson and the 180-mark contribution goal.

The Great Claim ledger now accepts three trade seals worth 40 marks each. Contribute materials you produced through mining, woodworking, metalworking or food production. Specialize or mix trades; the three seals are shared. Existing civic projects remain available and visibly improve town. A paid first shift and any two projects/seals earn your household plot. Registering a furnished home adds 60 marks: three seals plus a home reach the rescue milestone.

Report lessons at Stonebridge's forge, survey desk or Ivar's shift book for supplies and wages. Trade deliveries require both recorded work and carried materials. Rewards cannot be collected twice. The communal forge now makes the steel pick after Tempered steel research. Existing saves, houses and contest results persist; older finished inventory is not guessed to be player-made.

Human completion time and specialist/mixed-route pacing still need normal-speed playtesting. No deadlines or mandatory completion of every profession are added. The opening still ends with the Thane homecoming.

# 0.95.0 — The First Fortune

The opening chapter now continues from the miners' lodge through building your own home, uncovering your ancestry, proving your claim and earning the title of Thane.

After the royal reception, help prepare Keldrath's three wards and choose when to attend the ceremony. The interrupted investiture leads into seven playable defense fronts: fight invaders, escort familiar faces to refuge, operate the defenses and confront Skarrin. Short in-world scenes carry the King's intervention, judgment, investiture and homecoming. Your own house, workshops, possessions and relationships remain yours afterward.

This update also includes 36 optional friendship episodes across twelve characters. Errands and practical choices unlock brief scenes, useful rewards, experience and saved memories, including Maren's response to the rude miners. Begin courtship with a crafted Hearthknot. Alden and Maren's relationship advances when you commit to another partner, so waiting alone never closes those romance options.

## Start or continue

Extract **every file** from the Windows ZIP into a fresh folder. Run **FirstFortune.exe** beside **FirstFortune.pck**. Keep your previous build and saves.

For the complete new opening, choose **First visit to the hold** on the title screen and use an empty fortune slot. Meet Ivar in the bunkhouse and follow **J** for the next task. You can pause guidance, explore, work on your home or spend time with friends at your own pace.

An existing frontier fortune can continue its earned progress. Saves made after the royal reception receive the ward preparations. Older fortunes from before the frontier opening retain their original progression; a fresh slot is the clearest way to experience the full new chapter.
