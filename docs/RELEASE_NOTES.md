# 0.89.0 — A hearth of your own

Begin in the miners’ lodge, where Ivar and the other miners share the hearth and bunks. Ivar points you toward the private quarters through the lodge door and your first shift at Old Workings. Work a load at the stonebreaker to earn wages and materials, then return to your room and make it yours with a furnishing. The old cutters’ bay offers another lead when you are ready. Guidance is optional, and the longer path toward a homestead remains open.

From the lodge, leave for Hearthward, pass through the King’s Gate to the High Trail, and enter Old Workings. Press J for your journal and O in your room to place furniture. Your existing fortunes, furnished rooms and first-visit progress are preserved. Extract the Windows ZIP into a fresh folder and run **FirstFortune.exe**.
