# 0.95.0 — The First Fortune

The opening chapter now continues from the miners' lodge through building your own home, uncovering your ancestry, proving your claim and earning the title of Thane.

After the royal reception, help prepare Keldrath's three wards and choose when to attend the ceremony. The interrupted investiture leads into seven playable defense fronts: fight invaders, escort familiar faces to refuge, operate the defenses and confront Skarrin. Short in-world scenes carry the King's intervention, judgment, investiture and homecoming. Your own house, workshops, possessions and relationships remain yours afterward.

This update also includes 36 optional friendship episodes across twelve characters. Errands and practical choices unlock brief scenes, useful rewards, experience and saved memories, including Maren's response to the rude miners. Begin courtship with a crafted Hearthknot. Alden and Maren's relationship advances when you commit to another partner, so waiting alone never closes those romance options.

## Start or continue

Extract **every file** from the Windows ZIP into a fresh folder. Run **FirstFortune.exe** beside **FirstFortune.pck**. Keep your previous build and saves.

For the complete new opening, choose **First visit to the hold** on the title screen and use an empty fortune slot. Meet Ivar in the bunkhouse and follow **J** for the next task. You can pause guidance, explore, work on your home or spend time with friends at your own pace.

An existing frontier fortune can continue its earned progress. Saves made after the royal reception receive the ward preparations. Older fortunes from before the frontier opening retain their original progression; a fresh slot is the clearest way to experience the full new chapter.

## Playtest focus

The Thane homecoming is the stopping point for opening-chapter development. Test the journey's pacing, directions, combat difficulty and scene presentation; report where you became unsure what to do or had to repeat work. Save/reload and scene skipping preserve progress, while the duel response and trial opinion require an explicit choice.

Marriage and the next chapter's district development remain future work. Human completion time is not yet measured. Automated source and exported-package checks support this build; Windows hardware performance, audio and enjoyment still need normal playtesting.
