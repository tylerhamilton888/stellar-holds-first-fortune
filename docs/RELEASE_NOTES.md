## 0.125.4 · Player walking animation

Both boots now take a turn planting, pushing off and swinging through. The eight-pose walk follows distance traveled with empty hands or a selected tool, including the starting pick.

# 0.125.3 · Your first town quest

Ivar sends you to inspect Borin’s cold hearth for your first contest entry. The journal and on-screen directions guide you from the bunkhouse to the forge. Inspect the hearth to learn what it needs.

# 0.125.2 · Walkable stairs

Walk north up the stone treads or south to descend. You can stop partway and turn around. The lodge, household stairs, Keldrath service stairs and carved mine stairs all use movement. A darkened stairwell and a stone sill mark the view from above.

Ladders still use their existing climb keys.

## 0.125.1 · Stonebridge playtest fixes

From the main street, follow the south path to the fork. Turn west to read the town Ledger or take the eastern branch to Pinewood. At the Ledger, press **E** to open the projects. The **Ledger** tab lets you check them from elsewhere.

The Ledger now shows where you can deliver supplies and which goods are missing. Use **Clear filters** when a pack search turns up empty. The outdoor camera stays over painted ground, and full-size firs line the northern edge. Walking uses distinct passing poses and alternating boot contacts.
