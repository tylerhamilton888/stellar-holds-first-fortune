# Personal development — next design discussion

Current implementation: productive-work counters in Handworks, automatic shoulders/Miner unlocks, and nine technology methods with prerequisites. Existing values must survive any migration. The ancestral investigation introduces evidence-based research and civic standing separately.

Proposed next pass, not implemented: separate personal mastery (earned through meaningful work), knowledge (research/discoveries unlock methods), and standing (appointments grant responsibilities/permissions). Present one readable development screen with distinct branches, requirements and previewed effects. Avoid universal XP from every swing, payment for trivial repeated actions, and titles that somehow improve pick damage. Count successful production/discovery milestones, preserve earned progress, and favor handling, precision, recovery and organization alongside efficiency. Discuss branch choices and respecialization before replacing current unlocks.
