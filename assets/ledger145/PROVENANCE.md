# Civic ledger

Recovered original generated artwork approved in the November 7 Stellar Holds conversation: **Dwarven Civic Ledger on Gilded Stone Lectern.png**. SHA-256: `3f9a4caa0beee58dbebb760f6bf78a5c8ec0c740de1e2af71098a05a65fe21f3`. Copied byte-for-byte, with original transparency and artwork intact.

Owner direction: make the town ledger a large, beautiful, unmistakable object, inspired by the Great Book of Grudges. This painting depicts an open gilded book, red leather and ribbons, a carved stone lectern and Dwarf relief. The original generation prompt is not available in the recovered files.

Rendered at 180 × 180 world units using the shared furnishing catalogue, with a solid ground footprint and ordinary actor depth sorting. Two existing royal braziers and slate paving form the forecourt east of Stonebridge's bunkhouse. Contributions retain their existing state owners and the indoor records desk remains usable.
