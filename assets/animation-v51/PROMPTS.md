# Original animation and labor artwork — 0.51.0

Created with the built-in image generation tool for this project. Existing personalized Dwarf heads remain the identity source; new painted body poses are composited with those heads and recolored to the saved appearance. No third-party game images were copied. Original PNGs are preserved; `bounds.json` holds measured alpha-component regions. Atlas slicing, recoloring and head/hand foreground masks happen in Godot.

Files: male/female actions (drink, pick, hammer, axe), walk (four views/four phases), utility (shovel, saw, carry), handling (lift/carry rock and log), mining (north/south pick and hammer), garden (hoe, water, fish, harvest); dedicated drinks, strong-shoulders, pushing, idle, environment, geology, workshop, homestead-props and field-tools.

Earlier generation brief: headless stocky Dwarf bodies, teal work clothes, brown boots/backpack, broad inked contours, transparent separated pose cells; personalized head attached at measured neck landmarks. Geology uses continuous slate, stratified cut faces, dusty floor and deep crystalline granite, without separate boulder tiles. Environment contains compact tavern furniture, substantial terrace coping, geodes, ruins and a stairwell. These are prompt summaries; the retained exact final prompts follow.

Unused stride studies and superseded corrections are not runtime assets. Four-frame gait silhouettes still warrant further animation refinement; this collection is not a claim of a bespoke eight-direction cycle for every work action. Work and hauling generally use a mirrored three-quarter stance; pick/hammer also have front/back poses.
