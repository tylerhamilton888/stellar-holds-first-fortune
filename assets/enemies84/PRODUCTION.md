# Enemy artwork production — 0.74.0

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Selected final project assets: goblin.png, orc.png, troll.png. Three rows face down-right, right and up-right; left-facing variants mirror the complete pose. Six columns contain ready, two walk keys, anticipation, impact and fallen poses. Runtime regions and foot anchors are measured in scripts/enemy_roster.gd; do not assume equal cells. The complete bodies, hands and held weapons stay together.

Design briefs: an olive goblin cutthroat with burgundy hood, patched leather and a chipped cleaver; a muscular green orc with dark iron shoulder armor, burgundy sash and a two-handed axe; a slate-gray cave troll with mossy shoulders, hide loincloth and an iron-bound wooden club. Painterly fantasy strategy-game illustration, elevated camera, no anime/chibi/photorealism, transparent alpha. Target gameplay heights: 58, 80 and 110 world pixels respectively, compared beside the approved Dwarf.

The initial walking keys repeated a leading leg. Targeted image edits specified a raised image-left knee and planted image-right foot (and reversed rear/side contacts), preserving all other poses. A separate troll alpha repair removed background haze. Native extraction then revealed neighboring-frame fragments; each final sheet was regenerated with larger gutters using the exact prompt below, substituting its species for {species} and supplying that species' repaired sheet as the reference:

> Layout repair ONLY for this game sprite atlas. Preserve the exact 18 {species} character poses, all anatomy, opposite walking contacts, faces, colors, equipment and row directions. Keep canvas 1536x1024, SIX columns and THREE rows. Make EVERY sprite about 65 PERCENT of its current drawn size, and center each reduced whole sprite inside its own equal 256x341 cell, feet anchored at 80 percent cell height. This is critical: enormous empty transparent gutters around every sprite, at least 30 transparent pixels between each sprite and every cell boundary, including raised weapons and fallen bodies. No character, weapon or debris may spill into another cell. No stray fragments, no detached props except the existing fallen weapon beside each defeated body. Keep all 18 poses in the same order, six per row. Do not add or remove any sprite. Absolutely clear transparent alpha for all surrounding pixels and gaps. No ground shadows or haze. Do not paint an opaque background. We will enlarge each isolated sprite slightly in-game; clean isolation matters more than filling the sheet.
