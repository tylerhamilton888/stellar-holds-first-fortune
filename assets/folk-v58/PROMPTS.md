# Whole-character first drawings — 2026-10-19

Built-in ImageGen, transparent output. The user's `docs/art/reference-oct19.png` was the style/proportion reference. These are original complete-figure drawings, not a bake of the previous separated head/body rig.

Development art checkpoint, not a released replacement. Both core atlases contain 24 whole figures: four idle facings, four walking keys per facing and four side pickaxe keys. Alpha was measured; background RGB is present under transparent pixels. Never flatten it.

## Front correction study — not accepted as a complete walk cycle

Used male-core.png and female-core.png as identity references. The result holds frontal facing but repeats leg phases, so remains a study and is not selected by the development renderer.

```
Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: complete character animation sprites for The Stellar Holds.
Input 1 and 2 are reference images of our male and female whole-character Dwarfs. Draw a clean production animation correction sheet, NOT a character turnaround.
EXACTLY 4 columns and 2 rows: EIGHT complete figures. Top row the same male, bottom row the same female.
All eight face DIRECTLY TOWARD THE VIEWER, head, chest, belt and toes oriented down the screen. No side or three-quarter turns in ANY frame. Keep the entire face and both brows visible in all eight. Fixed orthographic top-down-game presentation, same body size and grounded baseline in each row.
The FOUR columns are four different keys of one FRONT walking cycle: (1) left boot forward planted, right foot behind; (2) left leg under pelvis bearing weight, right knee bent with RIGHT boot lifted toward viewer, clearly visible sole; (3) right boot forward planted, left foot behind; (4) right leg bearing weight, LEFT boot lifted toward viewer. Arms counter-swing; shoulders turn only slightly. Strong SHORT steps. Do not duplicate poses. Do not change costume, face, beard, hairstyle or proportions between frames.
Head, neck, broad shoulders, thick arms, short powerful legs, hands, boots and hair are DRAWN TOGETHER as one continuous coherent full body in every frame. No head layers or seams. Adult compact Dwarfs, not chibi, not anime. Keep source blue leather-braced male clothing and cream/teal female clothing. Male swept-back brown hair and short split beard with two small bronze tips. Female long thick braided brown hair behind shoulders. Warm skin consistent on face and arms.
Isolated on TRUE TRANSPARENT alpha with no shadows/background/text/labels/grid. Preserve clean inked outlines and warm hand-painted style. Generous clear space between figures. Landscape image.
```

## Male core

```
Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite atlas for The Stellar Holds, not a concept board.
Use the attached user concept as the sole style and proportion reference. Create ONE ORIGINAL COMPLETE ADULT DWARF in 24 full-body animation frames, on true transparent alpha. Head, neck, shoulders, torso, arms, hands, legs, boots, hair and any tool must be drawn together as one coherent figure in EVERY frame. No separated body parts, no portrait-sized pasted-on heads, no joint seams or skin-colored circles. Broad, powerful torso, stout short legs, large practical boots. Crown to chin (excluding beard) only about ONE QUARTER of total standing height. Adult proportions, NOT chibi, not anime, not lanky. Same warm skin tone on face, neck and arms. Head naturally nestled into shoulders.
Layout EXACTLY FOUR COLUMNS and SIX ROWS, 24 generously separated sprites, same body scale and ground baseline per row, no clipped limbs, no drop shadows, no labels, no background. High-resolution portrait atlas. Original hand-painted cartoony northern Dwarf style, dark clean ink outlines, readable volumes, restrained detailing.
Male identity: strong square face, large nose and heavy brows, dark cool brown swept-back hair and a SHORT SQUARE beard ending at upper chest, compact cohesive head. Blue/teal short-sleeved workshirt, brown leather belt/braces and bracers, navy trousers, brown boots, small brass fittings. NO giant mane or enormous beard hiding all the torso.
Rows:
1: relaxed empty-handed IDLE, views FRONT, RIGHT SIDE, REAR-RIGHT, BACK.
2: FRONT walking cycle, four distinct keys: left foot forward contact, left foot planted/right knee and sole visibly lifted passing, right foot forward contact, right foot planted/left knee visibly lifted passing. Whole body and arms participate; head remains part of figure.
3: RIGHT SIDE walking, same four distinct contact/passing phases, SHORT powerful strides with clearly different planted and lifted boots. Do not repeat a standing pose.
4: REAR-RIGHT walking, four contact/passing phases.
5: BACK walking, four contact/passing phases.
6: RIGHT SIDE PICKAXE work: low ready with both hands around shaft; raised behind-shoulder anticipation with visible fingers/shaft and engaged torso; low forceful contact to the right with iron pick tip at shin height; relaxed follow-through. Same dwarf face/hair/skin/proportions throughout. Tool drawn in hands, not an accessory floating near them.
Travel poses have a small practical belt pouch; hands empty, no random weapons or floating props. Keep face, hair arrangement and clothing consistent across all frames.
```
